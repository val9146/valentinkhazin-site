Val Khazin

I'm Val Khazin. This is a public page under my name, and a place to find writing published under that name.

The subjects are ordinary ones: travel, everyday technology, food, photographs, and other small practical topics. The notes are written in plain language and are meant to be useful without telling a life story. On travel, that includes packing a carry-on from a list written before the bag is open and closing it when the list is done, or walking the first hour in a new city with a simple map rather than opening five apps. On everyday technology, it means trying a tool you already have, such as a phone's offline notes app, before adding another. On photographs, it comes down to looking closely at a picture, stepping a little closer instead of zooming, and letting the edges of the frame cut away the clutter. On food, it means simple meals built from a few things that keep well: a grain, something fresh that does not bruise easily, and a flavor you already like.

I don't post a résumé here, and this page does not list a workplace or a location. If you searched the name, this is one of the public pages, and the writing is linked from here.
